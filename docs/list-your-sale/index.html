List your estate sale on The Dig

Estate sale companies will soon be able to list a sale on The Dig directly, for free. Direct listing is not open yet.

What it will be

  • Free. No listing fee and no commission.
  • On the map. Your sale appears on your city's map and list with its dates and hours.
  • Your address, your timing. Keep the street address hidden until closer to the sale. Shoppers see the area until you release it.
  • Linked to you. Every sale on The Dig links back to its own listing, so shoppers reach your photos and terms.

You may already be on the map

The Dig already pools sales from the public listing boards. If your sale is posted on one of them in a city The Dig covers, look for it here: Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, Austin, San Antonio.

What the message asks for

  • Company name and the sale title
  • Street address, and whether to hold it until closer to the sale
  • City and ZIP
  • Dates and hours for each day
  • A link to your full listing or photos
  • A contact name and phone number
