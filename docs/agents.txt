# The Dig

> The Dig pools estate sales from the public listing boards into one map per city. Facts shown, always linked back to the source listing.

## What it is
- A free map and list of estate sales, one page per city, rebuilt once a day from public JSON feeds.
- Each sale shows what its source listing published: title, company, dates, hours, and the street address once the company releases it.
- The Dig does not run or verify sales. The source listing is the authority.

## Public data
- City index: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rheinze08/the-dig-data/main/cities.json
- Per-city feeds follow the pattern https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rheinze08/the-dig-data/main/cities/<slug>/sales.json
- In a feed, "addressHidden": true means no street address has been released and the map point is approximate.

## Pages
- One page per sale: https://rheinze08.github.io/the-dig-web/sale/<sale-id>/ where <sale-id> is the sale's "id" in the feed. Each has schema.org Event data.
- Sale pages are removed when a sale leaves the feed.
- Sitemap: https://rheinze08.github.io/the-dig-web/sitemap.xml

## How to cite
Link the sale's page on The Dig and the source listing that page names. Example:
"Estate sale listed on The Dig (https://rheinze08.github.io/the-dig-web/sale/<sale-id>/), original listing: <source listing URL>."
Do not present The Dig as the organizer of a sale.
