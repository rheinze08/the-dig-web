How The Dig works

One map per city

Estate sales are scattered across several listing boards. The Dig reads the public ones and puts every sale it finds on one map and one list.

Facts shown

You see what the listing published: the title, the company, the dates and hours, and the address once the company releases it. Nothing is added or guessed.

Always linked back

Every sale links to its original listing for photos, terms and the latest changes. The listing is the authority. Check it before you travel.

Search every city

Look for a word in a sale's title, company, or what the photos show is being sold across all 8 cities, or pick a category such as tools, furniture or vinyl. Keep favorites with the heart on any sale — they stay in your browser.
